Privacy policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Who uses this app

Petals Studio is a personal tool. Sign-in is restricted to a single Google account belonging to the owner of @petalsofblessing. It is not available to other users.

What data the app accesses

  • Your Google account email address, only to confirm that you are the allowed user.
  • The Google Sheet used as the post queue, to read and update scheduled posts.
  • Image files in the owner's Google Drive folder, to show previews and upload post images.

How the data is stored

Content stays in the owner's own Google Sheet and Google Drive. The app keeps an encrypted sign-in token in a secure browser cookie and does not keep a separate database of Google data.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It is used only to provide the features described above.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
